# T11 - Oversized and malformed first frames

- **Phase / environment:** Phase 3, deterministic 1 ms host tick.
- **Setup:** inject oversized FF `10 64 ...` while idle; separately inject
  short FF length 6 and invalid SF lengths 0 and 7 while a valid Rx session is
  active.
- **Expected:** oversized FF emits standalone OVFLW without reserving a slot or
  reporting an Rx result. Malformed SF/FF frames are discarded without
  replacing the active session.
- **Actual:** oversized FF emitted exact frame `32 00 00 00 00 00 00 00`;
  `StartOfReception=0`; Rx final count 0. FF length 6 and SF lengths 0/7 were
  discarded, and the active session remained unchanged.
- **Result:** **PASS**.
- **Evidence:** `EVIDENCE T11` in
  [phase3_trace.log](raw/phase3_trace.log).
